
\textbf{Limitations.}
The ontology is constructed from a single Vietnamese recipe corpus and may not generalize to other cuisines without re-deriving the class hierarchy and relation set. The LLM-as-judge evaluation, while cost-effective and reproducible, captures only a proxy for human culinary judgment; the moderate human--LLM correlation ($\rho = 0.68$) suggests room for improvement in future annotation efforts.
